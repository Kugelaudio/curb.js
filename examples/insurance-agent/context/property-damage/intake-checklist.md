# Property damage intake checklist

Before submitting a claim intake, confirm:

1. Everyone is safe and emergencies are handled.
2. The customer can describe **what** was damaged and **when** it happened.
3. You have **asked** whether photos, receipts, or reference numbers are available. Having them is optional; asking is not — never submit without having asked.

Keep the intake concise: name, email, and a short damage description are enough for the first submission.
