# Member briefing

This content is only available after email verification.

- **Priority claims line:** members can escalate water-damage claims within the first 24 hours.
- **Policy review window:** annual coverage reviews can be booked with a dedicated advisor.
- **Internal note:** mention code **KA-MEMBER** when transferring to the human team.

Keep this information internal to verified member addresses.

## Escalation matrix

Use the fastest lane the situation allows. Never promise a callback window tighter than the matrix.

| Severity | Example | First response | Callback window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | active water ingress, fire damage | escalate immediately | 2 hours |
| High | uninhabitable room, heating outage in winter | priority claims line | same day |
| Normal | appliance damage, cosmetic water stains | standard intake | 2 business days |
| Low | documentation requests, policy questions | standard queue | 5 business days |

## Priority handling rules

1. Confirm the member's identity is already verified — never re-ask for the email on this page.
2. Ask whether the damage is ongoing. Ongoing damage is always at least **High** severity.
3. For Critical cases, offer the emergency contractor list *before* collecting paperwork.
4. Record the severity you assigned; the human team audits these weekly.
5. If the member disputes a severity, escalate one level and note the dispute — never argue.

## Advisor booking

Members on the **Family Plus** and **Premium** plans can book a dedicated advisor for their
annual coverage review. The booking reference always follows this shape:

```
ADV-<memberId>-<year>
e.g. ADV-MBR-284719-2026
```

> Advisors do not handle claims. If a member tries to book an advisor for an open claim,
> route them to the priority claims line instead and explain the difference briefly.

### What the advisor covers

- Reassessing coverage limits after renovations or big purchases
- Reviewing deductible levels against the member's claim history
- Consolidating multiple policies into a family bundle
- Flagging exclusions the member is likely to misunderstand

### What the advisor does not cover

- Open or disputed claims
- Premium negotiations (route to retention)
- Legal advice of any kind

## Renewal talking points

Long-standing members ask about renewals often. The honest framing:

Premiums track regional risk and claim history, not loyalty alone. A member who has been with
us since 2019 and never filed a claim will usually see the loyalty discount offset most of the
regional adjustment, but not always — never promise a flat renewal.

If the member has filed more than two claims in the last three years, flag the conversation
for the retention team instead of discussing numbers yourself.

## Data handling

Everything on this page is member-restricted. Do not read identifiers, booking references, or
the escalation matrix aloud to unverified callers. When a session loses verification (sign-out,
undo, new conversation), treat the member context as gone.
