# Shipping and delivery

Edit this file with your real shipping terms: Mara's answers hot-reload, no restart needed.

- Orders are packed within one working day.
- Standard delivery takes three to five working days.
- Shipping is free above an order value of 50 euros, and costs 4.90 euros below it.
- A tracking link is sent by email when the parcel ships.
- A parcel that has not arrived ten working days after shipping counts as lost.
